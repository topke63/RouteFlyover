# Sample data credits

The files in this folder are a ready-made demo trip over the **Stelvio Pass** (Italy):
drop all of them into Route Flyover at once to try the app.

## Route — `stelvio-pass.gpx`

- Road geometry from [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) contributors
  (ODbL), routed with the [OSRM](https://project-osrm.org/) demo server:
  Bormio → Passo dello Stelvio → Prato allo Stelvio, 46.7 km.
- Elevations from [Terrain Tiles on AWS](https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/)
  (Mapzen; SRTM, GMTED and other sources).
- Timestamps are made up (a riding pace that slows on steep sections), so the
  time and speed statistics are illustrative.
